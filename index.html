From observation
to movement.

The project explores goal-directed flight in simulated urban scenes. A policy observes the environment, chooses velocity targets, and learns from goal progress, obstacle proximity, altitude, and estimated power costs.

Observe

An 84 × 84 depth image, 64 LiDAR readings, a relative goal, and an estimated battery state form the core observation.

Learn

TD3 uses replayed transitions to update an actor and twin critics. Experimental extensions add curiosity and semantic features.

Move

The controller converts three velocity targets into simulated forces. V12 adds a penalty for changes between consecutive actions.

What the project covers

Urban scenes and obstacle awareness

The three scenario IDs load geometry from an external environment builder. Depth and LiDAR provide static obstacle observations. S3 includes wind and moving-object arrays whose connection to scene geometry is still under development.

Localization, path planning, and motion planning

Position comes from the simulator. A localization estimator would be a further extension. The learned policy selects local velocity targets. Global route search and explicitly timed trajectory optimization are additional planning components to explore.

Validation and current limitations

Both snapshots passed syntax and executable-structure checks after readability edits. Simulation execution requires the environment builder and drone asset. Distance norms, energy accumulation, reset observations, and experimental replay need correction before quantitative claims. V12's 30 m arrival radius also overlaps its easiest stage.

Research practice

The code was developed with AI assistance. The next experiments should connect source versions to configurations, checkpoints, training logs, and evaluation trajectories. The development notes record the outstanding implementation checks.
